## The offer

One offer for local businesses, said the same way by every caller, the email and the site.

**No website** (most of a caller's list: 128 of Rod's first 200)

- Free: a preview homepage with their name and trade on it, sent after they say yes on the call.
- $500 one time: the preview made real. Their name, number, hours, photos and Google reviews link, tap-to-call,
  on their own web address.
- Hosting included. The web address is in their name; they pay the registrar directly (about $10–20 a year).
- Bigger (up to 5 pages, hosting set up): $1,500.

**Has a website**

- Free: a check of what's costing them calls. Same report, but it leads with the top things that stop a new
  customer from calling (no tap-to-call on a phone, no contact form, site won't load or isn't secure, no reviews
  link). The score stays, lower on the page.
- $450 flat: the Fix Sprint, the same fixes, each said as what it does for calls.

**Bigger than a website** (law, accounting, anyone asking about AI)

- $197 remote or $297 in person: the AI Leverage Audit, as today.
